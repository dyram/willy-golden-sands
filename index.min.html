About Us

Committed to Quality & Innovation

Golden Sands of Abu Abali offers an outstanding range of GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic) products and related services in the Sultanate of Oman. Established in 2016, we are a customer-oriented firm committed to quality and complete customer satisfaction.

As a fully owned Omani company, registered in 2018, we specialize in manufacturing fiberglass products — paying meticulous attention to every product, ensuring performance and satisfaction for our valued customers.
